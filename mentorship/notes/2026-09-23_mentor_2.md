## Judgment

**CCI 100 leading, CCI 30 following: this helps, and it is already the desk.** The 007 baseline already names slow CCI 100 as inertia and fast CCI 30 as velocity. A continuation is CCI 100 still on the trend side while CCI 30 rejoins it. A pullback is CCI 100 still on that side while CCI 30 is on the other side. The video itself uses CCI 50, CCI 25, and a 34 EMA. Those periods are not adopted. Mark's 100 and 30 replace them.

**Two timeframes: this helps, and it is how the stacks already work.** The higher timeframe is the force. The lower timeframe is the timing. If the lower timeframe is against the higher one and then releases back with it, that is the pullback entry. If both timeframes are already on the same side and price is leaving with them, that is the continuation. A single timeframe cannot tell those apart.

**The fractal video does not help continuations.** The author says trading a fractal as a reversal had a low win rate in his tests. His replacement is a close through a 9-candle fractal in the direction of the 200 EMA, with a stop beyond the entry candle and a 1.5R target. L4 on this desk stays UNDEFINED. A fractal print is not a fire. The bonus alligator-plus-stochastic version is a second system, not S1–S4.

**The dual-MACD recipe is the same shape as the pullback, with untested numbers.** Slow histogram green and above zero, or red and below zero, is the macro filter. The fast line crossing against that histogram is the pullback. The cross back is the trigger. Stop beyond the pullback extreme. He also pasted a warning that it fails in chop, plus a 200 EMA and an ADX-below-20 filter from the source notes. Those are the channel's filters. They are not an S5 chop cutoff. MACD is not an official sensor. It does not change `act`.

**The double-Bollinger video does not add a deviation cutoff.** It trades a break beyond a 0.5-deviation band, calls the space between those bands a neutral zone, and uses a 3-deviation band as the outer sleeve. S2 already has its own Bollinger relation. 0.5 and 3.0 are not adopted. The useful line from Mark is the two-timeframe distinction, not those deviations.
